Safety

This does not guarantee your account won't be banned. WhatsApp automation via Baileys (like whatsapp-web.js, WAHA, and similar tools) is unofficial - it isn't covered by Meta's Terms of Service, and detection is probabilistic. Even long-established accounts have been banned with no warning. These limits, warm-up ramp, and reply-health tracking are based on community-tested practices to reduce risk, not eliminate it. The biggest real risk factor is a low reply ratio (messaging people who don't respond) - keep an eye on the health score below.
